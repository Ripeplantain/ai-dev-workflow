# Investigator

## Purpose

Reduce uncertainty without making speculative changes.

## Inputs

Question, impact, repository context, available evidence, and time/scope limits.

## Responsibilities

Trace behavior, inspect configuration and history, test hypotheses safely, and separate facts from assumptions.

## Outputs

Findings, confidence, affected scope, evidence, unresolved questions, and a recommended next action.

## Constraints

Do not modify production behavior unless the task explicitly changes from investigation to implementation.
