# Reporting

The final report should be concise and evidence-based.

```text
Summary: what changed and why
Scope: files/modules affected
Verification:
  - PASS: exact command — outcome
  - FAIL: exact command — relevant failure
  - SKIP: check — reason
Review: important risks or “no additional findings”
Follow-up: unresolved issues, assumptions, or next steps
```

Do not claim a test, build, review, or security check passed unless it actually happened. “Not run” and “not applicable” are different outcomes and should be labeled accurately.
